Stay grounded in an elegant existing atelier booking product. No generic SaaS blue, no neon, no gold luxury hierarchy, no thick ornaments. Distinct composition and identity rather than unequal privileges. Avoid long explanatory paragraphs or annotations inside the image.

## Карточки каталога

Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop catalog UI concept for Russian beauty booking platform «ателье».
Primary request: one landscape flat website screenshot that clearly compares THREE cards of equal WIDTH and HEIGHT in a single realistic catalog row. The three audience groups have different recognisable graphic compositions while sharing equal visual quality and booking convenience. This is a proposal, no production data or actual ratings.
Atelier brand palette: warm ivory #f5f1e8, deep forest #17352b, wine burgundy #74223e, subdued brass #b39460; fine borders, elegant serif headlines, modern readable sans-serif text. Calm editorial design, sophisticated but practical, generous breathing room.
Top navigation with wordmark «a ателье», links «Мастера», «Вдохновение», «Карта», minimal heart and profile icons. Heading «Найдите своего мастера». Simple search/filter row «Новосибирск», «Все услуги», «Дата», then EXACTLY three equally prominent cards.
LEFT card «Анна Белова» with type «Индивидуальный мастер»: distinctive personal aesthetic, warm burgundy/ivory framing with a single LARGE circular portrait of a fictional adult female specialist, face fully visible. One tasteful small photographic strip of real-looking manicure work, modest personal signature line. Independent masters must look just as polished as salons.
CENTER card «Салон Serein» with type «Салон»: clearly distinct studio/space aesthetic, rectangular warm salon interior photo integrated into forest-and-ivory architectural header; large crisp S logo tile and a compact team portrait strip. Beautiful understated salon brand, no VIP badge or larger booking button.
RIGHT card «Алина Миронова» with type «Мастер салона» and affiliation «В салоне Serein»: own recognizable staff card design, restrained forest border/brand stripe with a subtle brass line motif and ivory background. TWO LARGE CIRCULAR AVATARS of exactly equal diameter: female master photograph circle in foreground lower-left, salon S logo circle behind shifted upward/right, partially overlapping only 20-25 percent. Master face fully unobstructed and clear, master is the named primary identity; circles are clearly both large, not a tiny logo badge. This visual motif belongs to all salon employee cards, different people and salon branding can change. Add two small personal brow-work thumbnails beneath the portrait area to retain her own authorship.
All three cards have identical name typography size, equal photo-area footprint, exactly the same metadata positions and booking area: «4,9 · 28 отзывов», service category, «от 1 500 ₽», «Ближайшее время: завтра, 12:00», same burgundy «Записаться» button height, width and contrast and same heart save button. These are equivalent options in a neutral catalogue. Equal density, same baseline, fair visual weight. Distinct layouts in header areas, unified functional lower sections.
Accurate legible Russian Cyrillic, minimal amount of text; no grey disabled-looking cards, no premium/ranking markers, no extra side panels, no giant sale banners, no phone device mockups, no 3D/perspective, no watermark. Output should look like a considered implementable UI design, not a poster.
