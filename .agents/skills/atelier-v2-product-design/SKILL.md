# Atelier v2 product design

Use this skill when planning, reviewing, or changing the Atelier v2 user experience.

## Before making a proposal

- Read `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md` and `docs/V2_NEXT_IMPLEMENTATION.md` for the current state and work queue.
- Work on v2 only. Treat the approved Atelier concepts as the visual direction.
- Base proposals on actual product behavior and current screens. Do not describe a concept as implemented.

## Product and visual rules

- Make common tasks easy to understand, compact, and quick to complete.
- Keep salons and independent masters equally visible and premium.
- A salon page should express its space and team. An independent master's page should express the person and their work. A salon employee should remain identifiable as a professional with their own portfolio.
- Keep role differences clear: salon owners manage their teams; independent masters manage their own pages.
- Use the approved Atelier visual language: warm ivory surfaces, forest green, burgundy actions, muted gold accents, editorial serif headings, and clear sans-serif controls.
- Avoid scattered blocks, unnecessary whitespace, over-wide fields, and redundant buttons.
- Adapt desktop information hierarchy to mobile. Do not cause horizontal page overflow. Keep touch targets at least 44 px.

## Portfolio, catalog, and map

- Show common portfolio work in a Pinterest-like image grid: four columns on desktop and two on mobile.
- Provide useful filters, show each work's author, and link clearly to the author's page or booking.
- Keep the reading order understandable and provide pagination or an explicit “show more” action.
- Keep the map in scope for catalog work. Synchronize map markers, result cards, and active filters.
- Provide an obvious map/list switch and make the list comfortable to use on phones.

## Design approval

- Carry approved concepts into the real interface without requesting approval again for details within that direction.
- Before making a major change to information architecture or an approved visual direction, present a concrete proposal for review.s
